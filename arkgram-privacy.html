ArkGram Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-03-17

ArkGram is a native client for the Telegram network. To provide messaging, ArkGram strictly uses: Network (real-time connection), Microphone (voice calls/notes), Storage (media sharing). We process this data solely to deliver explicit communication services via the official Telegram cloud.

1. How we collect and use your personal information

We provide you with instant messaging, media sharing, and contact synchronization. To fulfill the requirements of providing this service, we process necessary information. If you do not provide the related information, your use of the core features in this app will be affected.

To implement these features, after obtaining your explicit consent, we need to collect and process your network connection information, precise and approximate location data, contact list details, microphone input, and media gallery access.

2. Required permissions

  • Internet: Required to connect to the Telegram servers to send and receive messages and media.
  • Vibrate: Used to provide haptic feedback for incoming messages and notifications.
  • Microphone: Needed to allow you to record and send voice messages to your contacts.
  • Read Media & Storage: Necessary to allow you to select, upload, and share photos and videos from your gallery within your chats, and save received files.

3. Third-party SDKs

Huawei Software Technologies Co., Ltd. - Push Kit SDK
Type of information collected: Push token, AAID (Anonymous Application ID), and basic device information.
Purpose: To establish a connection with the Huawei push server and deliver real-time push notifications for new messages and alerts.
Usage scenario: When the application receives an incoming message or notification in the background.
Privacy Policy: https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/en/privacy/

4. Managing your personal information

Accessing your information: Settings > Profile (profile details and uploaded media)

Deleting your information / Account: Settings > Account > Delete Account

Withdrawing your consent: HarmonyOS Settings > Apps > ArkGram > Permissions

5. Information storage location and retention period

Retention period: 0 days (Immediately). All local app data is erased instantly from the device, and your profile, messages, and associated data are permanently deleted from the Telegram cloud servers upon account deletion.

Server location: Europe (Telegram cloud infrastructure).
